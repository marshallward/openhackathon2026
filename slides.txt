MOM6 Solver
===========

* Modern Fortran codebase
* Fluid dynamics core
* Diverse physical parameterization solvers
* MPI parallelization
* **Bit reproducible** solutions

GPU strategy:

* ``do concurrent`` where possible
* OpenMP kernels where necessary
* Manual memory management

Proposal: ePBL
==============

Modern ocean mixed-layer parameterization

* Columnar loop structure (``j-i-k``)

* Dense subroutine call tree

* Iterative root finding

* Tridiagonal solvers

* Thousands of lines of code

Representative of our most challenging solvers
