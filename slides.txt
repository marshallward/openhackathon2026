MOM6 Structure
==============

Operational forecasting must remain intact

* Preserve the Fortran codebase
* Retain CPU performance
* Ensure **bit reproducibility**

Strategy:

* Fortran "``do concurrent``" where possible
* OpenMP kernels where necessary
* **Manual memory management**

Proposal: ePBL
==============

Modern ocean mixed-layer parameterization

* Columnar loop structure (``j-i-k``)

* Dense subroutine call tree

* Iterative root-finding

* Tridiagonal solvers

* Thousands of lines

Representative of our most challenging solvers
