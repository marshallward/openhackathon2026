.. figure:: media/mom6_relvort.mp4

A mission-critical model that doesn't run on GPUs.


Requirements
============

* Preserve the Fortran codebase
* Retain CPU performance
* Ensure **bit reproducibility**

Our strategy:

* ``do concurrent`` where possible
* ``!$omp target`` where necessary
* **Manual** memory management
